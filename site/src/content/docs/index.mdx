## One wizard. One manual stop.

YAMS Plus installs a private Jellyfin stack and configures the applications through their supported APIs. The one deliberate pause is yours: add at least one indexer you are allowed to use in Prowlarr. We do not guess credentials, choose questionable sources, or perform interpretive dance around copyright.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Fast by default" icon="rocket">
    Movies, series, subtitles, 1080p profiles, app connections and Jellyfin plugins arrive configured.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Modular on purpose" icon="puzzle">
    Add torrenting behind a VPN, Usenet over verified TLS, 4K movies, or Books beta only when you want them.

The public guide uses privacy-conscious, self-hosted Umami analytics to learn which documentation paths help people complete an install. It does not add analytics to YAMS Plus or any installed media service. [See exactly what the guide measures](/privacy/).

YAMS Plus is inspired by the approachable onboarding of [YAMS](https://yams.media/). It is not affiliated with YAMS and does not copy its website, screenshots or branding.
:::

## What you get

- Jellyfin, Seerr and Prowlarr as the always-on core.
- Radarr and Sonarr when movies or series are selected.
- Recyclarr-managed TRaSH profiles, including sane fallback and upgrade rules.
- Bazarr language profiles, SABnzbd or qBittorrent, and optional VPN kill-switching.
- A compatible Jellyfin plugin pack, audited again after restart.
- Encrypted backups, a stable JSON doctor report and an idempotent `apply`.

Start with the [requirements](/requirements/). Five minutes of reading now can prevent an intimate late-night friendship with filesystem permissions.
